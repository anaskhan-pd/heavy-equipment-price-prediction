01 / The Challenge

Heavy Machinery Resale & Auction Dynamics

Used industrial equipment (bulldozers, excavators, loaders) represents high-value, illiquid capital assets. Transparent valuation modeling eliminates underwriting friction in asset leasing, fleet disposal, and clearing price discovery.

Prediction Goal & Formulation

  • Target Variable: TargetValue, representing the final clearing sale price in USD across industrial equipment transactions.
  • Evaluation Metric: Root Mean Squared Logarithmic Error (RMSLE). Penalizes relative percentage variance rather than raw dollar differences.
  • Mathematical Formulation: All regression algorithms are fit on z = log1p(TargetValue) using standard MSE loss, directly optimizing competition RMSLE without heuristic post-processing.

Key Engineering Hurdles

  • Extreme Feature Sparsity: Over 40 attribute columns contain substantial missingness (optional hydraulics, stick attachments, enclosures).
  • Multiplicative Depreciation: Equipment wear compounds non-linearly across chronological age, operating meter hours, and physical machine scale.
  • High-Cardinality Codes: Highly specific manufacturer codes required leak-free Out-of-Fold Bayesian Target Encoding.

Why Log-Space Formulation Was Decisive

Because RMSLE(y, ŷ) = RMSE(log(y+1), log(ŷ+1)), converting target prices into log-space allowed tree algorithms (XGBoost, LightGBM, CatBoost) to directly minimize the evaluation metric at every split. Predictions are inverted back to USD via expm1.

06 / Engineering Takeaways

Key Learnings & Practical Principles

Technical principles established through cross-validation discipline and competitive modeling iterations.

01 / Metric Alignment

Log-Space Optimization

Formulating regression on log1p(y) transforms standard RMSE into exact RMSLE minimization, ensuring every gradient tree split aligns with competition evaluation.

02 / Leakage Prevention

Strict Out-of-Fold Encodings

Target encoding high-cardinality features within the same fold results in catastrophic validation leakage. Computing Bayesian statistics strictly out-of-fold was vital.

03 / Model Family Diversity

Ensemble Error Uncorrelation

Combining histogram-based LightGBM, depth-wise XGBoost, and symmetric-tree CatBoost produced uncorrelated residual vectors, enabling Level-2 Ridge stacking to lower RMSLE from 0.199 to 0.196.
